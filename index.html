Our Story

Café Aroma began as a single roasting drum in the back of a rented shopfront, run by two friends who thought the city's coffee was moving too fast to actually taste.

"We wanted a place where the wait for your order was part of the pleasure, not the cost of it."

A decade later, the drum is bigger and the shopfront is ours, but the pace hasn't changed. We still roast in small batches, still greet regulars by name, and still believe a good cup deserves five unhurried minutes.
